[-f.105v-] This Tretis is contriuid vpon the Gamme for hem that wil be syngers. or makers. or techers For the ferst thing of alle thei must knou hou many cordis of discant ther be. As olde men seyen. and as men syng nouadayes ther be .9. But whoso wil syng manerli and musikli. he may not lepe to the .15the. in no maner of discant. for it longith to no mannys voys. and so ther be but .8. acordis aftir the discant nou I vsid. And whosoeuer wil be a. maker he may vse no mo than .8. And so ther be but .8. fro vnisoun vnto the .13the. But for the Qvatrebil syghte. ther be .9. acordis of discant. the vnisoun. 3de. 5te. 6te. 8te. 10the. 12the. 13the. and 15the. Of the whech .9. acordis .5. be perfite. and .4. be imperfite. The .5. perfite be the vnisoun. 5te. 8te. 12the. and 15the. The .4. imperfite be the .3de. 6te. 10the. and 13the. Also thu maist ascende and descende with al maner of cordis excepte .2. acordis perfite of one kynde. As .2o. vnisouns. 2o .5tis. 2o .8tis. 2o .12this. 2o .15this. With none of these thu maist neyther ascende neyther descende. But thu must cunsette thes acordis togedir and medele hem wel. as I shal enforme the. Ferst thu shal medle with .a. 3de. a .5te. with .a. 6te. a .8te. with a .10the. a .12the. wit. a .13the. a 15the. Vndir the whech .9. acordis .3. syghtis be conteynyd. The Mene syght. The Trebil sight. The Qvatrebil syght. and other also of thi .9. acordis. hou thu shalt hem ymageyn betwene the playnsong and thi discant here foluythe ensample. Ferst to enforme a. childe in his countirpoynt. he most ymagyne his vnisoun. the .8te. note fro the playnsong benethe. his .3de. the 6te note benethe his .5te. the 4the. note benethe. his .6te. the 3de. note benethe. his .8te. euyn with the playnsong. his .10the. the 3de. note .a.boue.
