[-242-] This tretis is contriuid vpon the gamme for hem that will be syngers or makers or techers.

For the ferst thing of alle thei must knou hou many cordis of discant ther be. As olde men seyen and as men syng nouadayes, ther be 9. But whoso wil syng manerli and musikili he may not lepe to the 15the in no maner of discant, for it longith to no mannys voys, and so ther be but 8 acordis aftir the discant nou jvsid. And whosoeuer wil be a maker he may vse no mo than 8, and so ther be but 8 fro vnisoun vnto the 13the. But for the quatrebil syghte, ther be 9 acordis of discant: the vnisoun, 3de, 5te, 6te, 8te, 10the, 12the, 13the, and 15the. Of the whech 9 acordis 5 be perfite and 4 be inperfite. The 5 perfite be: the vnisoun, 5te, 8te, 12the, and 15the. The 4 inperfite be: the 3de, 6te, 10the, and 13the. Also thu maist ascende and descende with almaner of cordis excepte 2 acordis perfite of one kynde, as 2o vnisouns, 2o 5tis, 2o 8tis, 2o 12this, 2o 15this. With none of these thu maist neythir ascende neythir descende, but thu must cunsette thes acordis togedir and medele hem wel, as I shal enforme the. Fferst thu shal medle with a 3de a 5te, with a 6te a 8te, with a 10the a 12the, with a 13the a 15the. Vndir the whech 9 acordis 3 syghtis be conteynyd: the mene syght, the trebil sight, the qvatrebil syght. and othir also of thi 9 acordis,

how thu shalt hem ymageyn betwene the playnsong and thi discant here foluythe ensample. Ferst to enforme a childe in his counterpoynt, he most ymagyne his vnisoun the 8te note fro the playnsong benethe, his 3de the 6te note benethe, his 5te the 4the note benethe, his 6te the 3de note benethe, his 8te euyn with the playnsong, his 10the the [-243-] 3de note aboue, his 12the the 5te note aboue, his 13the the 6te note aboue, his 15the the 8te note aboue the playnson[g]

[Power, TreatiseMe, 257,1; text: 10, 12, (Gamut)] [POWERTR2 07GF]

Also for a 12e with a 10e, v[t], my, re, ffa, my, sol, ffa, la is plainsong.

[Power, TreatiseMe, 257,2; text: 10, 12, (Gamut)] [POWERTR2 07GF]

For twelfe, tenthe, and ffiftenthe, re, mi, vt, mi, ffa, re, ffa, sol, my, sol, la, ffa is the plainsong.

[Power, TreatiseMe, 257,3; text: 10, 12, 15, (Gamut)] [POWERTR2 07GF]

For a twelfe and thirtenthe, the plainsong is la, sol, sol, ffa, ffa, my, my, re, re, vt.

[Power, TreatiseMe, 257,4; text: 12, 13, 1[3], (Gamut)] [POWERTR2 07GF]

For a twelfe and ffiftenthe, the plainsong is la, ffa, sol, my, ffa, re, my, vt, or ellis la, my, sol, re, ffa, vt.

[Power, TreatiseMe, 257,5; text: 12, 15, (Gamut)] [POWERTR2 07GF]

For a thirtenthe betwene 2o twelthis with a 10e, the plainsong is 4er la. 4or sol, 4or ffa, 4or mi, 4or re, 4or vt.

[Power, TreatiseMe, 257,6; text: 10, 12, 13] [POWERTR2 07GF]

For a thirtenthe and ffiftenthe, the plainsong is re, vt, my, re, ffa, my, sol, ffa, la, sol, or ellis la, ffa, sol, mi, ffa, re, mi, vt.

[Power, TreatiseMe, 257,7; text: 13, 15, (Gamut)] [POWERTR2 07GF]

For a fiftenthe with a 10e, the plainsong is vt, sol, re, la, and vt, ffa, re, sol, mi, la, also vt, mi, re, ffa, mi, sol, ffa, la, and vt, la.
